# Arquitectura musical — paleta cálida

Cuatro planchas conceptuales generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Referencias: Espacios sociales (1).jpg y Casitas (1).jpg. Sin personajes ni azules. Fondo crema opaco. No están recortadas ni normalizadas para una grilla de Unity. Las notas aparecen como ornamentación con relieve sutil; el tallado puede necesitar mayor profundidad en una revisión. Revisar perspectiva y uniones antes de producción.

## 01-piano

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE high-quality modular architectural TILESET CONCEPT SHEET for a 2D side-scrolling platformer, Music Below. Two provided images are reference sketches, NOT edit targets. Reference 1: monumental instrument-shaped social buildings, gramophone/concert structures. Reference 2: houses whose actual construction is piano, guitar and drums. Translate their charming architectural imagination into polished CalArts-inspired cartoon game art, bold clean BURNT RED outlines, simplified rounded shapes, flat 2-3 tone cel shading, no pixel art, no photorealism. CRITICAL COLOR PALETTE: follow buildings in sketches: luminous lemon yellow, golden yellow, honey ochre, pale warm cream, peach/tan, terracotta and dark brick/burgundy red outlines and shadows. Absolutely NO BLUE, cyan, green or violet. Ignore all blue characters and blue swatches in reference. No characters. Architecture must structurally evoke instruments, not merely generic castle with instruments pasted onto it. Ancient gigantic building walls and interior, but playful and inviting. Include occasional recessed carvings of musical notation: exactly five parallel staff lines with clear noteheads and stems carved INTO several stone wall panels, same stone material with subtle dark red incised shading, not floating notes or printed labels. Only about one third of wall panels have these carvings, leave plain repeatable variants. Composition: landscape asset sheet, orderly separated rows with generous gutters on uniform plain pale cream background. Top row: 6 modular square wall panels, including plain fill, edge/corner, and two carved staff variants. Middle: floor/platform strips left/middle/right with consistent flat walkable heights, column pieces and doorway/window modules. Bottom: a larger assembled monumental doorway/interior bay showing instrument-inspired silhouette, staircase, arch pieces and small broken masonry. Mostly reusable architectural pieces, NOT a village illustration or full landscape. Strict front orthographic elevation, no isometric views. No words, labels, borders, grid lines, watermark, logos, atmospheric backdrop or cast shadows. VERSION PIANO PALACE: piano-key cornices with cream and brick-red keys instead of black, alternating tall key-like wall pilasters, rounded piano-body arch, soundboard-style golden wall insets. A monumental piano-inspired hall entrance and short keyboard staircase. Lemon and cream primary, deep terracotta outlines. Strong simple playful silhouettes.
