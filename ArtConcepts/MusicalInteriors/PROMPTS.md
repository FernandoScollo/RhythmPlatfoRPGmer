# Muros interiores musicales

Generados con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Cuatro PNG RGBA con alfa: comprobadas muestras del fondo y ventanas (alfa 0), y pared sólida (alfa 253 de 255). Algunas muestras centrales coinciden con parteluces (alfa 249–253). No se verificó cada píxel; conviene revisar bordes y opacidad para producción. La vista previa del generador puede mostrar color RGB oculto bajo el alfa; usar el PNG con transparencia habilitada.

Planchas conceptuales frontales: falta recortar, normalizar dimensiones y verificar uniones antes de usar como Tilemap en Unity. No se modificaron escenas.

## 01-piano-interior

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a 2D side-scroller INTERIOR WALL modular tileset sheet, not exterior buildings. Reference sketches provided only for musical architectural motifs and warm palette. Camera precisely perpendicular to the inside back wall, strict flat front elevation, ZERO perspective, ZERO visible top surfaces, ZERO side faces, ZERO vanishing points. No houses, facades, roofs, exterior silhouettes or whole buildings. Clean CalArts inspired cartoon, confident burgundy outlines, simple flat cel shading. Palette only lemon yellow, honey gold, warm cream, ochre, peach sandstone, terracotta and dark brick red. No blue, green or purple, no characters. CRITICAL: actual RGBA transparency! ALL window openings must be completely empty transparent alpha=0 cutouts, including enclosed openings inside wall tiles. No glass, reflections, white/cream fill, black fill, sky, landscape or checkerboard painted inside windows. Surrounding gutters also transparent alpha=0. Opaque walls, frames and trim only. Arrange 12 separated rectangular modules in a tidy four-column three-row sheet of equal cell sizes. Row one: solid plain masonry fill, masonry with engraved five-line staff and musical notes, plain pillar-adjacent wall, ornate wall with carved staff. Row two: four complete opaque rectangular wall sections each with a LARGE fully transparent window cutout occupying 50 percent of section area; window frame firmly embedded into surrounding wall. Row three: four matching interior-wall sections with different transparent window designs and restrained musical ornaments. All rectangular wall modules have matching straight left/right boundaries and same cornice/baseboard heights for horizontal assembly. No assembled scene, no typography or labels, no ground shadow. Musical notes are occasional recessed carvings in stone, not floating icons. Prioritize large clean window openings and reusable indoor background wall modules, not props. PIANO INTERIOR: cream/gold square wall blocks, cornices of alternating cream and terracotta piano keys, shallow vertical key-like pilasters. Windows are tall rounded rectangles in pairs or triples, mostly unobstructed transparent panes, thin frame dividers only. Soft golden main walls and brick red carved notes.
