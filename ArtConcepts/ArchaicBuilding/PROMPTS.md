# Archaic building — four tileset concepts

Created with the built-in image generation tool from the user's three attached references. Opaque ivory backgrounds. Concept sheets, not sliced Unity assets: normalize dimensions, remove backgrounds and validate seamless joins before production. Some pieces use slight perspective and need adaptation for strict grid use.

## 01-palacio

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE polished modular TILESET CONCEPT SHEET for the walls and INTERIOR of an archaic gigantic building in a side-scrolling 2D music platformer. References: first attached image for monumental ruined arches, columns, carved friezes and ivy; second for weathered masonry, enormous doors and old pipes; third for gold trumpet-bell shapes and sage/ochre/coral palette. References only, do not copy screenshots or characters. CalArts-inspired modern cartoon style, bold clean dark outlines, simplified appealing shapes, flat cel shading, not pixel art, not realistic or 3D. Orthographic FRONT elevation, no isometric perspective. Sheet of clearly separated modular assets on plain solid pale ivory background, no atmospheric backdrop, no scene, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no watermark, no characters. Consistent scale and generous gutters. Include 6 square repeatable wall panels and outer corner variants; floor and ceiling strips with matching flat heights; column base/shaft/capital modules; arch segments and a complete tall arch doorway; two window modules; stairs, broken ledge and small rubble. Distinguish lighter walkable foreground ledges from muted background wall panels. Large readable shapes, light wear not dense texture. VERSION 1: IVORY RUINED PALACE. Cream limestone, terracotta inlay, plum shadows, restrained moss green ivy. Rounded Romanesque arches, thick elegant columns, aged golden carved medallions, cracked plaster revealing brick. Mostly architectural building parts, sumptuous but simple. Delicate trumpet-bell motif in column capitals.
